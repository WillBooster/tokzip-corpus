Finally, `Object` and `{}` means "any non-nullish value" rather than "any object
type". `object` is a good choice for a meaning of "any object type".

**Invalid:**

```typescript
let a: Boolean;
let b: String;
let c: Number;
let d: Symbol;
let e: Function;
let f: Object;
let g: {};
```

**Valid:**

```typescript
let a: boolean;
let b: string;
let c: number;
let d: symbol;
let e: () => number;
let f: object;
let g: Record<string, never>;
```
