That's why Vue ships with [official type declarations](https://github.com/vuejs/vue/tree/dev/types) for [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) - not only in Vue core, but also for [vue-router](https://github.com/vuejs/vue-router/tree/dev/types) and [vuex](https://github.com/vuejs/vuex/tree/dev/types) as well.

Since these are [published on NPM](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/vue@2/types/), and the latest TypeScript knows how to resolve type declarations in NPM packages, this means when installed via NPM, you don't need any additional tooling to use TypeScript with Vue.
