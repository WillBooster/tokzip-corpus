> ⚠️ Windows supports listening for `SIGINT`, `SIGBREAK`, `SIGTERM`, `SIGQUIT`,
> `SIGHUP`, and `SIGWINCH` (all except `SIGINT`/`SIGBREAK` go through libuv's
> Windows signal emulation).
