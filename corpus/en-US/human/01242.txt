Ensure the `key` attribute is present when passing iterables into JSX. It allows
frameworks to optimize checking the order of elements.

**Invalid:**

```tsx
const foo = [<div>foo</div>];
const foo = [<>foo</>];
[1, 2, 3].map(() => <div />);
Array.from([1, 2, 3], () => <div />);
```
